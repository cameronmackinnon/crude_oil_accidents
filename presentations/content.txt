# physical chemical properties of crude oil
- hydrocarbons
- by weight: lots of carbon, some hydrogen
- light, medium, heavy (API gravity - relative weight to water (10 degrees))
- sour or sweet - sour is more sulfur (https://www.britannica.com/technology/fuel-oil)
    - Sweet crudes have sulfur contents of 0.5 percent or less by weight, and sour crudes have sulfur contents of 1 percent or more by weight. 

- using the info above ^:
    - sour is more corrosive than sweet;
    - heavier affects how it flows - more pressure and energy required to pump the oil
